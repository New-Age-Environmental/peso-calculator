What each rate means

Paying by US card. Visa and Mastercard cards issued outside Argentina are usually converted near the "MEP" dollar, a legal market rate set by trading bonds. Your card statement shows the exact rate later.

Cash at a cambio. What an exchange house or "cueva" pays you for US cash, the buy side of the informal "blue" dollar. Clean, newer $100 bills usually get the best rate.

Bank exchange. What Banco Nación pays for dollars at the official rate. Lowest of the three, so pesos cost you more this way.

The "dólar tarjeta" (about 2,119) you may see quoted is the official rate plus a 30% tax charge on cards issued in Argentina. It does not apply to a US card.

Tip. About 10% is the custom in bars and restaurants, more for great service. A "cubierto" (table charge) on the bill is not the tip.
